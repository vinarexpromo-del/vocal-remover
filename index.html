FAQ

Common questions.

How do I remove vocals from a song for free?

Upload your track to SPLITR, select "Instrumental only" mode, and click Process. Our AI — powered by Meta's Demucs v4 — separates the vocals from the music in under 30 seconds. No sign-up, no payment required.

What audio formats does SPLITR support?

SPLITR supports MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG, M4A, and AIFF. You can upload up to 10 files at once and process them in a batch.

Can I use SPLITR to make a karaoke track?

Yes. Select "Instrumental only" to get a clean backing track with no vocals — perfect for karaoke, covers, and live performance. The output is a high-quality 320kbps MP3.

How do I extract an acapella from a song?

Choose "Vocals only" mode. SPLITR will isolate the vocal track and remove all instruments, giving you a clean acapella you can use for remixes, mashups, or sampling.

What is stem separation?

Stem separation is the process of splitting a mixed audio track into individual components — vocals, drums, bass, and other instruments. SPLITR uses the "4 stems" mode to do this, powered by Demucs, one of the most accurate open-source models available.

Is SPLITR really free? Are there any limits?

Yes, completely free. No account needed, no watermarks, no hidden fees. You can process up to 10 tracks per session. The output quality is 320kbps MP3 — the same as most paid services.

How accurate is the vocal removal?

SPLITR uses Demucs v4 by Meta Research, which consistently ranks among the best models on the Music Demixing Challenge leaderboard. Results are excellent for most modern tracks. Heavily reverbed or layered vocals may have minor bleed.

Can I use the stems commercially?

SPLITR gives you the technical stems — but the copyright of the original music still belongs to the rights holder. Using stems for public releases or monetized content requires a license from the original artist or label.

How does the AI voiceover work?

Switch to the AI Voiceover tab, type your text, choose one of six voices, and hit Generate. SPLITR uses Coqui TTS — an open-source neural text-to-speech model — to produce a natural-sounding voiceover in seconds. Output is a 192kbps MP3 ready to download.

Is the AI voiceover free to use?

Yes, completely free. No account, no limits, no watermarks. Just type your text and generate. Works great for podcast intros, YouTube narration, track drops, and any content that needs a professional voice.

How does the MIDI extractor work?

Upload any audio file and SPLITR uses Basic Pitch by Spotify — an open-source neural network — to detect pitches and convert them into a standard MIDI file. Works best on isolated instruments or vocals. Download the .mid file and open it in any DAW.

What DAWs support the MIDI files from SPLITR?

Any DAW that supports standard MIDI files — FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro, GarageBand, Cubase, Studio One, and more. Just import the .mid file and you can edit every note, change the instrument, or apply any VST plugin.

API
Base URL: https://vinarexpromo-del--vocal-remover-api.modal.run

Vocal Removal / Stem Separation

POST / — Upload an audio file and choose processing mode.

curl -X POST "https://vinarexpromo-del--vocal-remover-api.modal.run/" \ -F "file=@track.mp3" \ -F "mode=vocals" # mode options: vocals | instrumental | stems

Returns: {"status": "done", "files": {"vocals": "url..."}}

BPM & Key Analysis

POST /analyze — Analyze BPM, key, Camelot and energy of a track.

curl -X POST "https://vinarexpromo-del--vocal-remover-api.modal.run/analyze" \ -F "file=@track.mp3"

Returns: {"status": "done", "bpm": 128.0, "key": "A minor", "camelot": "8A", "energy": 75}

MIDI Extraction

POST /midi — Convert audio to MIDI file.

curl -X POST "https://vinarexpromo-del--vocal-remover-api.modal.run/midi" \ -F "file=@track.mp3"

Returns: {"status": "done", "url": "...", "filename": "track.mid"}

AI Voiceover

POST /voiceover — Generate AI voiceover from text.

curl -X POST "https://vinarexpromo-del--vocal-remover-api.modal.run/voiceover" \ -F "text=Hello world" \ -F "voice=sarah" # voice options: sarah | marcus | elena | james | aria | echo

Returns: {"status": "done", "url": "..."}
